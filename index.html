The house

A fragrance should be finished
before it is spoken about.

That belief is why there is so little here. No catalogue, no countdown, no promises we have not yet earned — only the work, and the patience it asks for.

When it is ready, this is where it will be introduced: what we have composed, the materials it was built from, and why each one is there. Until then we would rather say nothing than say it early.
